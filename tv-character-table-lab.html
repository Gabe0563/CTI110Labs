What Your Table Needs

Name your table characters.
Give it a column that serves as the primary key.
Include at least one column that stores whole numbers (e.g., a character's age).
Include at least one column that stores text (e.g., a character's name).

Beyond those requirements, the columns are up to you — add whatever fits your show (occupation, hometown, catchphrase, whatever makes sense).
